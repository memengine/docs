# Contact Us

Last updated: July 24, 2026

## Support

Email: support@memoryo.dev

Use this for product questions, account issues, billing questions, dashboard access, API setup, SDK setup, and Memory Passport help.

## Security Reports

Email: support@memoryo.dev

Subject line: `Security report - MemoryOS`

Please include affected URL or endpoint, steps to reproduce, impact, and any safe proof of concept. Do not access, modify, delete, or exfiltrate data that does not belong to you.

## Privacy Requests

Email: privacy@memoryo.dev

Subject line: `Privacy request - MemoryOS`

Include the email or account identifier related to the request. We may need to verify identity before acting.

## Business and Partnerships

Email: support@memoryo.dev

Subject line: `Business inquiry - MemoryOS`

## Legal Notices

Email: support@memoryo.dev

Subject line: `Legal notice - MemoryOS`

Formal notices may require additional delivery details in a signed contract.
